# Manufacturer attribution audit

This pass does not constitute online verification or primary-source research:
online search was disabled. All proposed maker records are explicitly marked
externallyVerified:false in manufacturer-register.js.

- Total original catalogue: 2,000 stable card IDs.
- 1,544 already had manufacturer information.
- 448 blank entries now get a named representative historical producer or
  a manufacturer supported by the catalogue family association.
- 1,992 of 2,000 catalogue cards now have nonempty manufacturer names.
- Eight remain unresolved rather than guessing.

Remaining: Armscor VR80, Armscor VR60, Armscor VRF14 (import brand versus
actual factory); Type 2 Aircraft, Type 89 Aircraft, Type 97 Tank (arsenal
ambiguity); G 41 (G41(M) versus G41(W)); MP 3008 (many contractors).

These candidates are not individually verified against manufacturer archives.
Where multiple factories historically existed, some names explicitly identify
a representative producer, not every producer. Country flags are unchanged.
The register fills only blank starter model fields. Previously auto-saved
blank starter records recover their default, while user-edited values, images,
local/Drive references, names, descriptions, IDs, and other apps remain intact.
