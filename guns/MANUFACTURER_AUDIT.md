# Manufacturer attribution audit

This pass does not constitute online verification or primary-source research:
online search was disabled. The 448 earlier model-family proposals remain
externallyVerified:false. The user supplied a screenshot of a general
Armscor company profile, which identifies the brand-side company, **not**
the actual factories producing individual VR-series shotguns.

- 2,000 existing stable card IDs.
- 1,544 cards originally had maker text.
- 448 originally blank records have representative manufacturer candidates.
- 3 additional Armscor shotgun records now show an explicitly qualified
  **brand-side company**, rather than pretending that a manufacturer was
  confirmed: Armscor VR80, VR60, VRF14.
- The generic G 41 now shows **two conditional makers**: Carl Walther for
  G41(W) and Mauser for G41(M). Its original model name does not identify
  which subtype was actually made.
- 1,996 / 2,000 cards show some company information (including three
  brand-only and one dual-variant listing). These are not 1,996 individually
  verified factory records.
- Four remain fully blank: Type 2 Aircraft, Type 89 Aircraft, Type 97 Tank
  (Japanese arsenal not identified), and MP 3008 (multiple late-war makers).
- Eight original factory-specific identification questions remain when the
  three brand-only Armscor models, the generic G 41 model and four blank
  records are counted together.

The three brand-only references display:
"Armscor Global Defense, Inc.（ブランド元／実製造元未確認）".
This is a company identification attributed to the user-provided screenshot,
not evidence that this company or a factory in the Philippines physically
manufactured these specific VR models. Their country flags remain blank until
the model's manufacturing site is independently verified.

The user supplied a screenshot from a G.41 German small-arms search result,
in which Walther's constrained production capacity is mentioned. That
snippet does not distinguish G41(W) from G41(M). The Walther/Mauser
model-variant split has not been independently verified against original
manufacturer records in this offline pass. The second screenshot lists
MP 3008's origin as Nazi Germany, not an individual manufacturer or factory:
its manufacturer and maker-country flag are intentionally still empty.

Stable IDs, data order, photo references, descriptions and source=user manual
edits are preserved. Automatic legacy starter-value recovery applies only
where a saved card's source is starter, without overriding explicitly edited
or intentionally cleared user fields. Source-level verification against
manufacturer documentation remains outstanding.
